i. Manifesto

Upper Capital was founded by entrepreneurs, for entrepreneurs. Since 2012, we have placed conviction behind more than one hundred and fifty seed and growth-stage businesses — companies building the rails of what comes next.

We bring the experience of operators — scar tissue, instinct, pattern recognition — and the resources to accelerate traction and growth.

— The partners

We do not write large checks late. We write early checks with patience, and we stay close. Most of the founders we back come to us through other founders we've backed — a quiet network that prefers signal to noise. 1

1.

Last year, more than 70% of our new investments were sourced through existing portfolio founders. We consider this our most reliable filter.
