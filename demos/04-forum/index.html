§ I — Manifesto

Upper Capital was founded by entrepreneurs, for entrepreneurs.

We've spent fourteen years writing first checks into companies we believe in — and staying close enough to be useful long after the round closes. Most of the founders we back arrive through other founders we've backed. That's not an accident. It's the operating system.

We bring the experience of operators — scar tissue, instinct, pattern recognition — and the resources to accelerate traction and growth.
